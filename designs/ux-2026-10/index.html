RoomDrop / front-end UX review / October 2026

Two light, mobile-first directions for the drop flow

Companion previews to docs/ux-review-2026-10.md. Both fix the review's P1 findings: positive verbal savings, no floating control over prices or buttons, consent feedback at the point of decision, and a desktop layout that uses the viewport. Fictional data only; no live integrations.

A · The Statement

A calm financial statement. Slim sticky booking strip, three comparison rows that fit one phone fold, tabular positive savings, receipt-first review, risk panel welded to the action bar, adviser as an inline lane. Desktop adds a summary rail.

Open direction A full screen ↗
  • All three options visible in one 844px fold.
  • Room name is the headline; hotel stated once in the strip.
  • Blocked approval explains itself inside the action bar.

B · The Concierge

Guided, one option at a time. A reasoned hero recommendation, a segmented switcher for the alternatives, an on-demand compare-all table, and a three-step review where consent and the approve button share a step. Adviser is a docked half-sheet with its grounding shown.

Open direction B full screen ↗
  • Shortest path to a confident first decision.
  • Consent lives in the same step as approval, so blocking cannot be missed.
  • Desktop becomes a master–detail two-pane.
