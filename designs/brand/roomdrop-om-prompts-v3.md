Single centered horizontal logo, comfortable margins, crisp flat solid-color vector-style graphic, no gradients, shadows, texture, labels, tagline or mockup. Make a beautifully designed custom typographic ligature, immediately readable as RoomDrop.

Preserve all dark letters "Ro" and "Drop", their typography and position, the coral palette and ivory background. Preserve the second o as a coral rounded-square key head with square hole.
